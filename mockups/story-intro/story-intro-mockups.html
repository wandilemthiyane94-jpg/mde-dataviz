Mockups · not the live site

No phone number in the story

The red button reads "They called for help" and only moves the story on. A page that looks like a flood-help service must never show a number that doesn't work. A checked helpline can go on a final card later.

Our own figure, real moves

The yellow family pin replaces Google's Pegman. The four moves are the ones GroundUp reported for families from Mega Village (2019 to 2025), with their real dates. The family is drawn, not named.

Fix in the film before using it

At 20 s the animation says "Three children are swept away" inside this family's journey. That loss was Lulama Dingiswayo's, and reports don't say she made these moves. These mockups leave that frame out.
