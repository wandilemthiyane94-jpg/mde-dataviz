How it plays

Film sections play by themselves. Red buttons are the reader's clicks. Every move shows the film's rope lifting the family, then the route on the real map.

Whose story is whose

The drawn family stands for families of the Gwala Street camp; their moves follow GroundUp's reporting. The children who died were Lulama Dingiswayo's, at that camp, and the loss slide names her.

No phone number

"They called for help" moves the story on. A page that looks like a help service must not show a number that doesn't work.
