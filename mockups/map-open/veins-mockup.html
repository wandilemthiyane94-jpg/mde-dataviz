How it moves

It opens as a black-and-white survey sheet: 40 m contours, the coastline and a lat/long grid. The 12 relocation sites drop in, each labelled. Then the city's own rivers and 1-in-100-year flood plain grow inland from the coast like veins; each site within 250 m of the flood line pulses red as the water reaches it. Green terrain colour fades in, then real satellite colour last.

On or off

The Animation switch turns motion off and shows the finished map at once (and it is off automatically for anyone whose device asks for reduced motion). Replay runs it again.

What is real

Every pixel of water is the city's real flood plain and river network, and the sites are their real positions. The growth from the sea is an animation of the map appearing, not a flood simulation.
