    Datasets at a glance

    • Site list: “Temporary Relocation Areas (TRAs) in South Africa”, compiled 27 Sep 2026 from 2022–2026 online sources. 84 sites, each with sources. data/raw/South_Africa_TRAs_2022-2026.xlsx → data/tra_sites_full.csv
    • eThekwini Flood Plain 100yr (municipal open data) and 300 seeded random points. data/tra_sites_exposure.csv, data/raw/durban_random_baseline.csv
    • JRC Global Surface Water, occurrence 1984–2021 (Pekel et al. 2016). prototype/story/film-data.js
    • World Weather Attribution (2022): the KwaZulu-Natal rainfall attribution.
    • Reflooding search: 30 of 84 sites, with evidence and confidence. data/raw/tra_reflood_sweep.json
    • Global Dataset of Planned Relocation Cases (PDD, Kaldor Centre, IOM; Leaving Place, Restoring Home I and II): 408 cases, of which 64 were sampled at random and coded. data/world_relocation_coded.csv, data/world_relocation_test.csv
    • World Bank income groups. data/raw/wb_country_covariates.csv
    • 11-case root-cause matrix. data/relocation_rootcause_matrix.csv
    • Published research and reports: Bower et al. 2023 (Nature Climate Change); the Auditor-General's 2022 flood relief report; eThekwini to Parliament (8 Mar 2023); The Durban Coalition, From Crisis to Coalition.
