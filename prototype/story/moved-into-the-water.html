Does anyone get it right?

Moved, and dry

Grantham, 2013 flood: 3 homes damaged in Grantham; 119 were destroyed across the Lockyer Valley in 2011. The other four sites have no flooding reported since people moved in, but none has yet faced a flood as big as the one that displaced them. Every success also has caveats: Grantham helped only property owners, and Gramalote took six years.

Sources: Grantham, Okada et al. 2014, IJDRR; Vunidogoloa, McMichael et al. 2019 and 2021; Iwanuma, Japan Reconstruction Agency; Overdiepse, Roth & Winnubst 2015; Gramalote, Natural Hazards Center and resettlement plan.

Data and method

Sites: "South Africa TRAs 2022–2026" list (84 sites, with per-site sources), transcribed to data/tra_sites_2022_2026.csv.

Satellite water: JRC Global Surface Water occurrence 1984–2021 (Pekel et al. 2016, Nature 540: 418–422), global-surface-water.appspot.com.

Floodplain: eThekwini Municipality, Flood Plain 100yr, eThekwini open data.

Reflooding: the site list's status notes; IOL, Lamontville Feb 2025; Mail & Guardian, May 2022; GroundUp.

World cases: data/raw/relocation_deep_*.json. Key sources: Coelho (Caravan) and IRCDUC for Chennai; Lauer et al. 2026 for Kasiglahan; IOM (2010) for Corail; Okada et al. 2014 for Grantham; Reconstruction Agency for Iwanuma; Oliver-Smith and Arenas for Gramalote; Roth and Winnubst 2015 for Overdiepse; McMichael et al. 2019 for Vunidogoloa.

Scoring: data/relocation_rootcause_matrix.csv. Eleven cases chosen because of how they turned out, so read the result as a pattern, not a statistical test.
