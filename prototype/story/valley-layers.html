The same 10 km of Durban, pulled apart. Ground, height, the city's own flood line, the April 2022 flood, the water that feeds it, and where people were placed. Read it from the bottom up.

Layers, bottom to top. Existing ground: Sentinel-2, 29 March 2022, with terrain shading. Height above river: our relative elevation model (AWS Terrain Tiles, IDW method). City flood line: eThekwini Flood Plain 100yr. Flood: our rain-on-grid simulation at 03:00 on 12 April 2022 (ERA5 rain plus upstream river inflow; indicative depths).
