Bars: the wettest three days of each year at the camp's location, ERA5 reanalysis (Open-Meteo), 1940–2025. ERA5 is a coarse grid and underestimates short local downpours. The 86-year record shows no statistically significant trend (Mann–Kendall z = 0.62); what stands out is the recent cluster of very large storms. Family floods: April 2019 (245 mm), April 2022 (233 mm), February 2025 (46 mm). Attribution: World Weather Attribution (2022), KwaZulu-Natal floods.
