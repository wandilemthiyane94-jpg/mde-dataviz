Map 2 · actors

Who holds what

Government is split three ways and joined only by an intergovernmental forum. National warns and classifies, the province shelters, the municipality draws the flood line and finds the land. The neighbours' objection (NIMBY) reaches the land decision; the flood victims' voice does not.
