Illustration of one group of families' journey, 2019–2025 (people and buildings shown are illustrative, not portraits). Events: GroundUp (Jun 2022, Mar 2025), Mail & Guardian (9 May 2022), The Witness (10 Jul 2025), Amnesty International (Nov 2025), Sunday Tribune (5 Oct 2025); our site map. Gauge: ERA5 hourly rain at the camp (Open-Meteo), which smooths short local downpours; "a normal month" = 82 mm (1991–2020); pools = rain on the uMlazi's 905 km² catchment, assuming the same rain fell across it (order of magnitude; 1 pool = 2,500 m³). Water levels in the scene are illustrative. We ran our rain-on-grid flood model for all three storms (2019, 2022, 2025), but at 33 m resolution it cannot resolve the camp: the camp's own cell sits above the modelled water while the neighbouring cells are the canal (up to 3.2 m deep in 2022). Only the rainfall on the gauge is measured data.
