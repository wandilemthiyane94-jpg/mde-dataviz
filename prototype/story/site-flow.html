04 · Design rules

How it should feel

One map, many scalesEvery story beat is the same map zoomed to a new scale. Visitors never lose their place.

People before pointsA site opens on its people (households, years waiting) before its flood risk.

Grey means unknownMissing data stays visible as grey, never hidden and never guessed.

Restraint at the lossThe three children are named only as the record names them: no faces, no reconstruction beyond the film.

Two gates, two questionsThe "why" screen ends on the two questions from the world test: who decides, and what can stop an unsafe move.

Phone first for the portalThe people who can fill the gaps use phones and WhatsApp, and mostly isiZulu.
