Schematic illustration. Events: GroundUp (Jun 2022, Mar 2025), Mail & Guardian (9 May 2022), The Witness (10 Jul 2025), Amnesty International (Nov 2025), Sunday Tribune (5 Oct 2025); our site map. Rain: ERA5 hourly reanalysis at the camp (Open-Meteo), which smooths short local downpours. 1 bucket = a normal month of rain here (82 mm, 1991–2020). Pools: rain on the uMlazi's 905 km² catchment upstream of the camp, assuming the same rain fell across it (order of magnitude; 1 Olympic pool = 2,500 m³).
