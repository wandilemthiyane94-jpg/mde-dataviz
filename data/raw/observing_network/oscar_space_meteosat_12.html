Satellite: Meteosat-12 

Satellite details
Acronym Meteosat-12
Full name Meteosat-12 (formerly MTG-1)
WIGOS Station Identifier 0-20009-0-071
Satellite Description
  • 1st flight unit of MTG, 1st of the “I” type.
  • Mission: operational meteorology.
  • Contribution to space weather monitoring
Mass at launch 3600 kg Dry mass 1600 kg
Power 2000 W
Data access link https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/mtg-in-operations
Data access information
  • No direct broadcast of data
  • Near-realtime Data distribution to users by EUMETCast or through dedicated ground lines or the RMDCN/GTS.
  • Alert messages of GEOS&R to LUT in real time.
Orbit Geostationary orbit Altitude 35786 km
Longitude 0.3° W

 

Space agency EUMETSAT , EC, ESA
Status Operational
Details on Status
(as available)
  • MTG-I1 commissioning until 4 Dec 2024 when it became Meteosat-12.
  • Parallel operations of Meteosat-10 (prime) and Meteosat-12 until June 2025.
  • Parallel operations of Meteosat-12 (prime) and Meteosat-10 until Q2 2027.
  • From Q2 2027 Meteosat-12 (prime) with Meteosat-10 as backup.
  • Lightning imager data operational since 31 October 2024
  • FCI L1C operational since 4 Dec 2024.
Launch 13 Dec 2022 EOL ≥2033
