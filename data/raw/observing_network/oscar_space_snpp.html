Satellite: SNPP 

Satellite details
Acronym SNPP
Full name Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership
WIGOS Station Identifier 0-20009-0-224
Satellite Description
  • Prototype of the JPSS programme, originally designed as Preparatory Programme of the aborted NPOESS (National Polar-orbiting Operational Environmental Satellite System).
  • Main mission: operational meteorology.
  • Substantial contribution to atmospheric chemistry and climatology.
Mass at launch 2128 kg Dry mass 1400 kg
Power 2285 W
Data access link https://data.noaa.gov/onestop/collections?q=snpp
Data access information
  • Real-time availability of ATMS, CERES, CrIS, OMPS and VIIRS by HRD.
  • CrIS data distribution ceased Sept 2024 due to lack of LWIR channels
  • Offline data are available from CLASS.
Orbit Sunsynchronous orbit Altitude 824 km
ECT 13:25 asc
