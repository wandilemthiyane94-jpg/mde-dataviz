Satellite: Meteosat-9 (IODC) 

Satellite details
Acronym Meteosat-9 (IODC)
Full name Meteosat-9 (IODC)
WIGOS Station Identifier 0-20009-0-056
Satellite Description
  • Meteosat-9 moved to 45.5E in 2022 to become Meteosat-9 (IODC)
  • 2nd flight unit of the Meteosat Second Generation programme.
  • Main mission: operational meteorology.
  • Substantial contribution to climatology.
Mass at launch 2040 kg Dry mass 1070 kg
Power 600 W
Data access link http://www.eumetsat.int/website/home/Data/DataDelivery/EUMETCast/index.html?l=e
Data access information

Near-real time availability of all image data by EUMETCast. More info at:http://www.eumetsat.int/website/home/Data/DataDelivery/EUMETCast/index.html?l=e
Orbit Geostationary orbit Altitude 35786 km
Longitude 45.5° E

 

Space agency EUMETSAT , ESA
Status Operational
Details on Status
(as available)
  • Meteosat-9 has been relocated over the Indian Ocean.
  • The launch date should be understood as the date of the satellite becoming operational at the position of 45.5°E.
  • The SEVIRI, GERB and DCS instruments are activated at new location.
Launch 01 Jul 2022 EOL ≥2027
